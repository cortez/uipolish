✦ Agent skill

Your UI works.
Now make it feel finished.

uipolish is an agent skill that runs a systematic polish pass over your codebase — microinteractions, spacing, typography, depth, and interaction states. It audits first, applies concrete recipes instead of vibes, and never changes behavior.

$ npx skills add cortez/uipolish
01

Recon & audit

Detects your stack, tokens, and conventions, then builds a punch list of everything that feels off.

02

Typography & spacing

Tracking, line-height, rhythm, and grouping — hierarchy you can feel without reading.

03

Surfaces & depth

Layered shadows, quiet borders, radii that nest correctly, elevation that survives dark mode.

04

Motion & microinteractions

Hover, press, enter, exit — transform and opacity only, guarded by reduced-motion.

05

States & verification

Loading, empty, error, focus. Then a diff review, keyboard walk, and your own build.

It sharpens what’s already there. No redesigns, no new dependencies, no behavior changes, and accessibility is never traded for aesthetics.
