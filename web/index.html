uipolish

Make your UI feel finished.

An agent skill that runs a systematic polish pass over an existing codebase: microinteractions, spacing, typography, depth, and interaction states. It audits first, applies concrete values with proven recipes, and never changes behavior.

npx skills add cortez/uipolish

Then run /uipolish in your agent, or just say “polish this UI.” It works in five passes:

  1. Recon & audit: your stack, your tokens, a punch list
  2. Typography & spacing: hierarchy you can feel
  3. Surfaces & depth: quiet borders, layered shadows
  4. Motion: transform and opacity only, reduced-motion safe
  5. States: loading, empty, error, focus, then verify

No redesigns, no new dependencies, no behavior changes. Accessibility is never traded for aesthetics.
